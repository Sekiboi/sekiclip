Sekiclip is an offline media editor (beta).

• Free forever — no paid plan, no watermarks
• Your media stays on this PC (nothing is uploaded)
• Installs only for your Windows user (no admin password)

This release includes the tools needed for video and audio.

After install:
  App → under your user profile (Programs\Sekiclip)
  Settings → kept separately so upgrades don’t wipe them

Preview may look softer than the file you export; final quality is what you save.
